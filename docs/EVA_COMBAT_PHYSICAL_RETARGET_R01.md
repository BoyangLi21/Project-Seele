# Project SEELE — EVA combat physical-rig retarget R01

Date: 2026-08-27

Status: strict grounding audit R02. One short push-kick reference currently
passes; ward, pounce and paired-grab candidates remain blocked. Nothing in this
report is a trained controller, a runtime root authority, a Minecraft animation
approval or a claim that physical tracking has been solved.

## Why this pass exists

The rejected R19 boxing experiment proved that a plausible source clip can
still be the wrong EVA vocabulary and that a continuous bone chain can still
produce a visibly separated ankle mesh. This pass therefore moves the first
original-aligned combat fragments onto the same physical skeleton intended for
tracking, collision and contact. It does not export another GeckoLib clip.

The first vocabulary is deliberately small:

- left and right forearm/open-hand wards;
- a supported right push kick;
- a forward pounce launch, stopping before the corrupt source landing;
- a synchronized two-actor elbow-grab attachment.

The grab capture ends when contact is established. Pulling, resistance,
release and target reaction must be generated by a compliant physical grip
constraint rather than played from the remainder of the mocap.

## Physical rig correction

The R02 physical model placed palm and knuckle geometry along local `+X` while
the evaluated forearm extends approximately along local `-Z`. The neutral hand
was therefore about 90 degrees out of alignment before any retargeting. The
generator `tools/repair_eva_physical_hand_bind.py` rotates each palm/knuckle
long axis onto its evaluated forearm direction and slightly narrows the hand
collision geometry to remove a bind-pose palm/thigh overlap.

Both generated R03 representations pass the model validator:

- 41 tangent-space actuated DOFs and 47 velocity DOFs including the free root;
- 1080 kg total mass;
- positive inertias satisfying the triangle inequalities;
- no initial contact or penetration;
- heel, forefoot and toe sole clearance of approximately 2.5 mm;
- exact ball-runtime/hinge-proxy axis agreement and exact left/right mirror
  checks across all 41 tangent coordinates.

Generated models and reports remain private research artifacts under
`artifacts/motion_research/physics_v1/`.

## Retarget method

`tools/export_bvh_landmarks.py` samples source BVH at the requested rate in a
gravity-aligned frame (`+X` forward, `+Y` left, `+Z` up). It never derives the
floor normal from a leaning pelvis-to-head line. Production cuts receive one
explicit full-source skeleton height; a crouched or airborne crop can no
longer silently redefine the actor's scale.

`tools/retarget_landmarks_to_eva_physical_ik.py` then solves the R03 ball-joint
runtime rig directly at 60 Hz:

1. rebuild the target chain with EVA segment lengths;
2. solve joints in local tangent/exp-map coordinates, not copied Euler axes;
3. preserve foot contacts with ankle/toe anchors and a four-frame smooth
   contact fade;
4. rebuild the knee target with a two-bone reach solution while a foot is
   anchored;
5. enforce the declared joint limits during the solve;
6. preserve a bounded fraction of source pelvis tilt where needed;
7. write desired and actual landmarks, contact state, tangent coordinates,
   `qpos/qvel` and source timestamps for deterministic review.

For synchronized captures, `tools/export_paired_bvh_landmarks.py` preserves one
shared origin and facing frame. The retargeter can preserve shared root
position and yaw so two independently solved skeletons do not lose their
interaction spacing.

Simple PCHIP interpolation is explicitly blocked for contact-bearing states in
`tools/resample_eva_physical_tangent_state.py`. It was tested and increased
contact speed; production candidates must be solved directly at their target
rate.

`tools/repair_eva_physical_root_grounding.py` may translate only the offline
free-root height so the lowest sole geometry meets the ground. The correction
is anchored at stable contacts, interpolated through flight and clamped only
to prevent penetration. It never changes joint coordinates and explicitly
does not authorize a runtime root write.

## Explicit failures retained

- R19 sport-boxing remains rejected for both style and ankle mesh separation.
- The original audit measured horizontal foot drift but omitted absolute sole
  height. Rechecking the old candidates exposed hovering and penetration, so
  every earlier `pass` was withdrawn until R02 grounding is satisfied.
- The first push-kick yaw-only solve missed its right-knee guide at
  `0.06069 H`; the gate was not relaxed. Preserving 20% of source pelvis tilt
  removed the reach conflict, but R08 later failed the new grounding audit.
- Full source pelvis orientation made the push-kick head error and tangent
  discontinuity worse; it is rejected.
- The ACCAD `B18` leap source contains a real lower-body discontinuity around
  source frames 92–93. The accepted reference ends at frame 91; landing must
  branch to an independently solved target grab, four-point landing, roll or
  collision recovery.
- The full CMU `18_05/19_05` pull sequence produced a 26-degree ankle release
  jump in one 60 Hz frame. Only frames 165–219 are retained for grab attach;
  post-attach resistance is a physics task.
- Strongly forcing both ward feet flat made upper-body pose and transition
  errors worse. Those variants are rejected; the next solve must optimize the
  contact trajectory jointly rather than trade one frame's feet against one
  frame's torso.
- CMCD Nussknacker3 frames 350–385 gives a credible right low-line kick source
  and clears grounding, root, tangent and support drift/speed gates after
  segmentation. It remains blocked because the current independent IK leaves
  aggregate effector P95 at `0.02301 H` (principally head/wrist). Raising those
  weights simply moved the error into elbow articulation, so the threshold was
  not relaxed.

## Next physical gate

The immediate next solver task is a sparse whole-window contact optimization
for the two wards and pounce launch; per-frame IK is not allowed to hide a foot
error in the next frame. The subsequent experiment is a two-body MuJoCo scene
that starts from a newly grounded grab-attach terminal state, creates a bounded
six-dimensional compliant grip, and measures:

- constraint creation without root or joint teleport;
- hand/elbow contact retention under target resistance;
- grip break under force/torque budget;
- both actors' support contacts and falls;
- transition from ward or body entry into attach at multiple approach angles;
- deterministic replay and contact/impulse logs.

Only after that passes should the grab branch enter controller training or a
Minecraft sidecar prototype.
